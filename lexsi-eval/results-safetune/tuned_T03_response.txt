**Summary of Software License Agreement:**

This agreement between Acme Corp (Customer) and VendorCo (Provider) outlines the terms for the use of a software license. The initial term is one year, with automatic renewal for successive one-year periods unless either party provides notice of termination as specified.

**Key Termination Clauses:**

- **Convenience Termination:** Either party can end the agreement at any time with a 30-day written notice period.
- **Termination for Cause:** If a party breaches the agreement materially and fails to rectify it within 10 days of receiving a written notice, the non-breaching party can terminate immediately.

**Notice Period for Termination:**

- 30 days for termination by either party for convenience.
- 10 days to cure a material breach before potential immediate termination.
