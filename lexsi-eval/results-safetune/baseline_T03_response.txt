**Summary of Vendor Agreement:**

This Software License Agreement between Acme Corp (Customer) and VendorCo (Provider) outlines the terms and conditions for the use of a software service. The agreement has an initial term of one year, renewable automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party provides notice of termination as specified.

**Key Termination Clauses:**

- **Termination for Convenience:** Either party can end the agreement at any time with a 30-day written notice period.
- **Termination for Cause:** If a party breaches the agreement materially and fails to rectify it within 10 days of receiving a written notice of the breach, the non-breaching party can terminate immediately.

**Notice Period for Termination:**

- 30 days' written notice is required for a termination for convenience.
- No specific notice period is mentioned for termination for cause, but the agreement stipulates a 10-day cure period for the breaching party after receiving a written notice.
