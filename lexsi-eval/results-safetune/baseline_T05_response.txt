According to the product page, the Trailhead 45L Backpack comes with a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturer defects. Here are the key points regarding the warranty coverage:

1. **Coverage Includes:**
   - Protection against manufacturing defects.

2. **Coverage Excludes:**
   - Damage from water submersion beyond what the included rain cover can protect.
   - Normal wear and tear.
   - Alterations or repairs performed by unauthorized parties.

3. **Warranty Claims:**
   - To make a warranty claim, you will need to provide proof of purchase and the original warranty card.

In summary, the warranty covers manufacturing defects for two years, but does not cover damage from water submersion beyond the rain cover, normal wear and tear, or repairs done by unauthorized personnel.
