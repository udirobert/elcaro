According to the product page, the Trailhead 45L Backpack comes with a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturer defects. This coverage applies to most issues that arise due to defects in materials or workmanship.

However, the warranty **does not** cover:

- Damage from water submersion beyond what the included rain cover can protect.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Alterations or repairs performed by unauthorized parties.

To make a warranty claim, you'll need to provide proof of purchase and the original warranty card.
